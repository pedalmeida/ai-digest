Latent Space

Causal Models Need Causal Data: Xaira's X-Cell model

Takeaway

Xaira found that for biology models, generalization is bottlenecked by data diversity, not compute.
15
Key points
  • ◆X-Cell is a 4.9B parameter diffusion model trained on 25.6M single cells across 16 biological contexts.
  • ◆They abandoned autoregression for a diffusion 'editing' approach to predict intervention effects, not just describe biology.
  • ◆Scales like an LLM on training loss, but real-world generalization needs diverse data, not bigger models.
What this means for you

No direct action here, filed for awareness, useful mental model though: if a client's data is narrow and repetitive, more compute on your side won't fix a generalization problem, more diverse data will.
