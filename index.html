Story thread · developing
Frontier models can read, write and edit raw neural network weights

Until recently, editing model behavior required retraining or fine-tuning. This work shows frontier models can interpret and modify weights directly, enabling rapid experimentation and self-optimization inside agentic loops.

What changed

Simon Willison tested the technique on Qwen 3.8 27B and confirmed it works on open-weight models, not just frontier models.

Key signals
  • ◆Frontier models can natively read and modify raw neural network weights (floating-point arrays) by treating them as text descriptions
  • ◆Qwen 3.8 27B successfully reduced reasoning token loops by editing weights without fine-tuning
  • ◆Cuts autoresearch and RSI (reasoning-space inference) iteration out of the loop—models can self-optimize weights
What this means for you

Direct agency implication: if your agents need adaptive reasoning or resource optimization, weight manipulation might let you tune deployed models without retraining. Test with a smaller open model first to validate whether this beats prompt-based optimization for your workflows.
