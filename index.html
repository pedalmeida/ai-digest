Latent Space

Exo: Harnesses should see their own code and logs

Takeaway

The next wave of agent design gives the agent visibility into its own code and logs so it can self-improve safely.
13
Key points
  • ◆Exo can clone itself and manage a lineage of clones, but an immutable event log prevents it from erasing its own history
  • ◆Framed as a shift in the ML stack: from model weights mattering most to agent harness design mattering most
  • ◆Distinguishes implicit self-improvement (memory updates) from explicit (rewriting its own prompts, tools, or policy)
What this means for you

No direct action here for the agency this week, but the harness-over-weights framing is the right mental model to keep evaluating vendor claims: a client doesn't need GPT-6, they need a well-designed harness around whatever model you pick.

Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social (Best of the Pod)

Takeaway

Sarah Tavel thinks the next big consumer AI win comes from adding social and network effects to single-player AI tools.
14
