Andrej Karpathy, Ethan Mollick
Builder signals, practitioner reports

Karpathy highlights output interpretation as limiting factor; hidden reasoning traces push toward automation over augmentation.

🟡 Watch
05
Story thread · developing
LLM output interpretation is now the bottleneck; reasoning traces disappear for anti-distillation

As models improve, two opposing forces emerge: (1) companies want to protect reasoning from distillation, (2) builders want to inspect and guide in-progress reasoning. The trace removal is winning, pushing practices toward pure automation.

What changed

Karpathy released prompt engineering trick (ASD-STE100); Mollick published friction report this week.

Key signals
  • ◆Karpathy: ask LLMs to explain outputs in ASD-STE100 (controlled language spec) to improve clarity
  • ◆Mollick: removal of detailed thinking traces from Claude and ChatGPT makes human-in-the-loop harder
  • ◆Shift is intentional (anti-distillation + UX) but drives devs toward full automation rather than augmentation
What this means for you

This is a friction point for your agency. If you're building human-in-the-loop workflows for clients, you lose visibility into model reasoning. Counter: ask Claude to output structured reasoning in a lightweight format before final answer. Test ASD-STE100 for client-facing explanations.

Recommended action

investigate, how to adapt your agent workflows to work without detailed reasoning traces. Consider structured intermediate outputs as a workaround.

Why this made the cut: Directly affects how you build augmentation products; signals a design shift in frontier models.
