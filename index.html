AT&T
Enterprise AI case study, via AINews

AT&T routes 40% of employee AI usage to open models, cutting coding costs 56% with only 2% quality drop.

🟢 Apply now
03
Key signals
  • ◆Target is 60-70% open-model routing at 45 billion tokens/day.
  • ◆Hybrid routing (open model first, frontier model as fallback) is becoming the default enterprise pattern, not the exception.
  • ◆Quality loss is nearly negligible at this scale, undercutting the case for always using frontier closed models.
What this means for you

This is the exact pitch structure for your AI agency: sell PT SMBs a hybrid setup where cheap open models (Qwen3.8-27B, GLM) handle 80% of tasks and a frontier model is called only when needed, this is your cost story in sales calls.

Why this made the cut: Concrete enterprise numbers, rare in a week of vague model releases.
