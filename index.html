  • ◆Enables bypassing traditional fine-tuning; weights become a programmable interface
What this means for you

For your AI agency: this cuts manual model tuning time. You can now prompt-edit open-weight models (Qwen) to optimize for SMB workflows—faster inference, lower latency. Test with a small client task this week.

Realtime-Bench is a new evaluation framework that measures how well models perform on tasks with hard latency constraints (e.g., game responses, live bidding). It reveals that even GPT-6 cannot reliably respond within the time windows required by many real-world applications.
