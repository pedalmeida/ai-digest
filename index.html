Decision Models as Emerging Standard Category

Decision models are purpose-built to output deterministic structured answers (yes/no/choice/score) rather than free-form text. This emerged ~Nov 11 with Jev, now Google and OpenAI have entered. It signals the model market is segmenting beyond general-purpose reasoning.

What changed

Google's entry confirms this is becoming a standard category, not a one-off from one lab.

Key signals
  • ◆Gemini Decision 1 supports yes/no, choice, and rubric-scoring questions; reuses Jev's "Noul" terminology
  • ◆Priced at same cost as GPT-6 Luna (~$0.10/M input tokens), suggesting commoditization
  • ◆Three major labs (OpenAI, Anthropic via Jev, Google) now offering decision-specific models signals category maturity
What this means for you

If your SMB agency builds agents that make structured decisions repeatedly (lead scoring, customer routing, workflow triage), decision models could reduce costs and improve reliability vs. using general-purpose models for the same task. Test one workflow that makes 100+ decisions/week.

Recommended action

watch - Test with one SMB workflow that makes high-volume structured decisions. See if decision model beats general model on cost and consistency.

Why this made the cut: Only real model category entry today; signals market segmentation shift.
